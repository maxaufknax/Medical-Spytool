Getting Started

Start by going to the Search page:

  • Enter keywords or search terms
  • Select a database (PubMed or Deutsche Nationalbibliothek)
  • Use advanced options for date ranges and additional filters
  • Associate searches with specific people for better organization

After your search completes, you'll be directed to the Results page:

  • View your search results in table or card format
  • Filter results using the search box
  • Sort by different columns
  • Export results to CSV or Excel for external analysis

Visit the Analysis page to visualize your results:

  • View publication distribution by year
  • See top authors in your results
  • Compare database distributions
  • Analyze citation patterns
